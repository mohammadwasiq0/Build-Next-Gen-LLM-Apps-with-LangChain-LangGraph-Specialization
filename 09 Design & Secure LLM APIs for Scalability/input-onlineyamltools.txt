strategies:
  - name: Balanced Data Sampling
    stage: Data Collection
    description: >
      Ensure demographic groups are proportionally represented by using
      stratified sampling and synthetic data augmentation where necessary.
    expected_impact: >
      Reduces data imbalance, improves fairness during model training,
      and minimizes the risk of majority-group bias.

  - name: Explainability Integration
    stage: Modeling
    description: >
      Use model-agnostic explainability tools such as SHAP or LIME to
      generate clear reasoning behind predictions and allow teams to
      detect biased decision patterns.
    expected_impact: >
      Increases transparency for stakeholders and provides interpretability
      needed to assess fairness and accountability requirements.

  - name: Post-Deployment Bias Monitoring
    stage: Deployment
    description: >
      Implement ongoing monitoring pipelines to measure fairness metrics,
      detect drift, and identify emerging biases in real-world data.
    expected_impact: >
      Ensures long-term fairness, provides early warning signals when
      model behavior changes, and supports regulatory compliance.

  - name: Human Oversight Workflow
    stage: Deployment
    description: >
      Introduce human review for high-risk predictions and maintain
      structured escalation processes for cases with potential harm.
    expected_impact: >
      Enhances accountability, prevents automated errors from escalating,
      and builds trust in system decision-making.
